## Example D — Omitting ephemeral beats

Source material covers a street-naming story, then a brief reaction to a social media post, then a summit anecdote.

The post reaction is omitted. The outline includes only:

```
The Street-Naming Auction
The Summit Chair Incident
```

## Setting up a chapter

Brief segues and one-liners belong inside the chapter they set up — not as standalone headings.

A short intro before a segment on zoning reform stays part of **Zoning Reform in Austin**, not **Opening Remarks**.
