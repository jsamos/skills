# Upshot Examples

Illustrative only. Always derive from the actual source.

## Example A — Upshot (good)

**Source:** Taste Labs segment, Diet TBPN transcript

**User:** upshot on the AI slop backlash

**Output:**

AI apps look samey ("slop"). Taste Labs sells design data labeling so frontier labs and startups can train models to output better-looking UI. The launch got mocked ("you can't program taste"), but the hosts think customers will still buy that service near-term — long-term, less clear.

**Why it works:** Problem, concrete product, reaction, verdict — no assumed context.

## Example B — Upshot (bad)

**Output:**

You can't reliably codify taste, but everyone still hates AI slop — so there's real demand to make AI output look less generic, even if "Taste Labs" is the wrong framing.

**Why it fails:** "Wrong framing" is unexplained; "demand" doesn't say who pays for what; logic contradicts itself.
